## 2026-04-17 - Standardizing Form Accessibility and Feedback
**Learning:** Initial MVP components lacked basic accessibility (labels, focus states) and async feedback (loading indicators). Adding these small touches significantly improves perceived performance and usability without requiring major redesigns.
**Action:** Always associate labels with 'htmlFor', provide 'focus-visible' rings for keyboard users, and use animated spinners like 'Loader2' for long-running operations.
