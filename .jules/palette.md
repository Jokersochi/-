## 2025-05-15 - [Form Accessibility and Visual Affordance]
**Learning:** Connecting labels via `htmlFor` and `id` is crucial for keyboard accessibility and screen readers, but adding visual icons (visual affordance) significantly reduces cognitive load by quickly identifying field types at a glance.
**Action:** Always pair accessibility (ARIA/labels) with visual cues (icons) and provide explicit feedback for async states (animated loading spinners).
