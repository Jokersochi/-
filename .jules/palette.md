## 2025-04-07 - Accessible Form Labels and Visual Affordance
**Learning:** Linking labels to inputs via `htmlFor` and `id` is a fundamental accessibility win that is often overlooked in MVP-stage React applications. Adding icons (e.g., Lucide) to labels and buttons significantly improves visual affordance and makes the interface more intuitive.
**Action:** Always verify that labels are correctly linked to their inputs and consider adding icons to primary actions and form fields for better UX.
